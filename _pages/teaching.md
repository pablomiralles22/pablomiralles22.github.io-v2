#### 2024-25
- **Generative Models** (_Master in Deep Learning_). Course on generative models, covering Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), autoregressive text generation and diffusion.
- **Deep Learning for NLP** (_Master in Deep Learning_). Full introductory course on the application of Deep Learning techniques to real world problems. We focus on the use of the HuggingFace library (pipelines, datasets, auto models, trainer) for different problems (classification, generation, semantic search). We also introduce LangChain, LangGraph, the vLLM library, vector databases/indices like Faiss...
